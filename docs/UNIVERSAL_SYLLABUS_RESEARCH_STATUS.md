## 1. Rules for research status

An exam is not marked fully verified merely because an official URL is found. Full verification requires reading the applicable official syllabus document, checking its syllabus pages, identifying all stages/papers/subjects, and recording exact source-page references.

Statuses used in this register:

- **CONTENT_READ** — relevant official document/syllabus text has been opened and read; structured normalization or final metadata QA may remain.
- **PARTIAL** — only part of the official source or syllabus has been read.
- **DEFERRED_NOT_BLOCKING** — research is incomplete, documented transparently, and will not stop work on the next exams.
- **PENDING** — research has not yet been completed.

Third-party copies may help locate documents, but they are not accepted as proof of official syllabus content.
